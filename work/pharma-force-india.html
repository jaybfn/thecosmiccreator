Pharma Force India.

A Karnataka-wide distributor of WHO-GMP branded generics, selling to pharmacies, hospitals, clinics and individual buyers at the same time — with nothing online to prove any of it.

The challenge

Pharma Force distributes WHO-GMP certified medicines across Karnataka. Trust is the entire product — but nothing about their digital presence earned it.

The harder problem was audience. A hospital procurement officer, an independent pharmacist and a person looking for a specific medicine all needed to arrive at the same site and immediately find their own path. Most distributor sites pick one and lose the rest.

The approach

We positioned the brand around the three things buyers actually judge a distributor on: availability, quality and distribution reach. Everything else came second.

From there we split the site into two journeys — institutional B2B and individual B2C — running on one set of trust signals. Brand partners and therapeutic categories do the credentialing; WhatsApp-first enquiry keeps the last step as short as the way the business already works.

What we created

  • Brand positioning & messaging framework
  • Full website design & build
  • B2B and B2C journey architecture
  • Therapeutic catalog pathways
  • Brand partner & trust signal system
  • WhatsApp-first enquiry flows

The result

A live site that carries the full catalog story — 2,000+ medicines across 9+ brand partners — with institutional credibility on the front page and a clear route to enquire from every section.

Pharma Force can now be checked out before a first conversation, which is exactly what a distributor in a trust-led category needs.
