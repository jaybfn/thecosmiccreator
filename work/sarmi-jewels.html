D2C jewellery · Pan-India

Sarmi Jewels.

Modern 925 sterling silver, made for everyday wear. Refined in the hand — but the brand around it hadn’t caught up with the product yet.

Industry
D2C fashion & jewellery
What we did
Brand identity · Ecommerce storytelling · Social · Content
Live at
sarmi.in

The challenge

Jewellery is bought on feeling and confirmed on trust. Sarmi had both covered in the product — hallmarked, authenticated 925 silver — and neither coming across online.

In a category crowded with identical product grids, nothing separated the brand from a marketplace listing. Discovery was happening on social; conversion had to happen on the storefront; the two weren’t telling the same story.

The approach

We built the brand around everyday elegance rather than occasion luxury — silver you wear on a Tuesday, not silver that lives in a box.

Then we made the proof do quiet work instead of shouting: BIS hallmarking, free pan-India shipping and authentication became part of the story rather than a badge strip. Collections got narratives, not just names, so a browser has something to remember the brand by after they close the tab.

What we created

  • Brand voice & positioning
  • Ecommerce storytelling & collection narratives
  • Product-led visual direction
  • Instagram & Facebook content strategy
  • Ongoing social creatives & campaigns
  • Trust & authentication messaging

The result

A live storefront at sarmi.in where the brand reads as consistently as the jewellery looks, and an active social presence built for discovery rather than decoration.

Product, storefront and social now run on one voice — which is what turns a first purchase into a second one.
