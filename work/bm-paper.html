BM Paper. The full stock.

Twenty-five years of wholesale stationery from Sultanpete — paper, cartridges, government supplies — with a digital presence that didn’t show any of it.

The challenge

BM Paper is the kind of business procurement officers already know by phone — 10,000+ SKUs, GST-clean billing, same-day dispatch across Bengaluru. Online, it looked like any other paper shop.

The harder problem was audience. A government tender desk, an MNC stores team and a neighbourhood printer all buy from the same counter. A site that spoke to only one of them would lose the rest.

The approach

We stopped selling “paper” and started selling the position: one stationery partner who can fill the whole office, not a vendor for a single line item.

Range became the proof — categories, government and corporate pathways, a full catalogue. WhatsApp-first bulk enquiry kept the last step as short as the way the business already works.

What we created

  • Brand positioning & site narrative
  • Full website design & build
  • Product catalogue architecture
  • Government & corporate pathways
  • WhatsApp-first bulk enquiry

The result

A live site that carries the full stock story — paper through packaging, government stationery through corporate supplies — with a clear route to enquire from every section.

Twenty-five years of counter reputation now reads as one wholesale partner, which is what a first-time buyer needs before they pick up the phone.
