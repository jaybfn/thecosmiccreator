The challenge

Srikanth’s work is behavioural as much as it is financial: stay invested when markets fall, treat volatility as the feature. That is a hard thing to prove on a brochure site, and most advisor pages look like the same three services and a phone number.

The audience is mixed — first-time SIP investors, families mid-correction, corporates booking a talk. One cold “about the advisor” page would flatten all of them.

The approach

We built the site around the volatility-coach position, not a generic wealth-manager template. The tools do the arguing: a SIP planner that makes compounding visible, a risk quiz that names the investor’s temperament before anyone sells a fund.

Media, talks and WhatsApp sit at the edge of every section, because the conversion is a conversation — the site’s job is to make that conversation feel inevitable.

What we created

  • Personal brand positioning
  • Full website design & build
  • SIP wealth planner
  • Investor risk profiler
  • Media, talks & enquiry architecture

The result

A live site where a prospect can run the numbers, read the philosophy and book a conversation without needing a prior introduction.

Twenty-three years of coaching now has a front door — which is what a practice built on trust needs when the next client arrives from a talk, a reel or a search.
