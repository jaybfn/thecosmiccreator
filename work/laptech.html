LAPTECH. One partner.

Fifteen years of IT infrastructure expertise — hardware, surveillance, networking, power, fire safety — reading online like a list of unrelated vendors.

The challenge

Breadth was LAPTECH’s biggest asset and its biggest communication problem. Nine solution lines across hardware, CCTV, networking, power and fire safety — presented as a list, it reads like a company that does a bit of everything and none of it especially well.

They were also selling to three audiences with nothing in common: homes, growing businesses and enterprises.

The approach

We stopped selling the catalog and started selling the position: one partner who takes responsibility for the whole building, instead of five vendors who each blame the other.

Every solution line was rewritten as a problem it solves. Industry use-cases let each audience recognise itself. The AMC and support story — usually buried in the small print — became a proof point, because in infrastructure the thing clients are really buying is who picks up the phone in year three.

What we created

  • Brand strategy & solution storytelling
  • Complete website design & build
  • Nine solution-line pages
  • Industry use-case architecture
  • Digital marketing brochures
  • Brand system for sales & marketing

The result

A live end-to-end site covering all nine solution lines with consultation-led calls to action, plus a brochure and brand system the sales team can take into a meeting.

Fifteen years of credibility now reads as one trusted partner — which is what it always was.
