Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026.

What this site collects

This website does not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies, and it has no contact forms. Nothing you do on these pages is recorded by us.

Fonts

Typefaces are served by Google Fonts. Loading them sends your IP address and browser details to Google, as described in Google’s privacy policy.

When you contact us

If you email, call, message us on WhatsApp or book a call through Cal.com, we receive whatever you send — typically your name, contact details and what you’d like to discuss. We use it only to reply and to work with you, and we don’t sell or share it with anyone outside the studio.

Booking a call is handled by Cal.com and WhatsApp messaging by Meta; each processes your data under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept while the conversation is active and for our ordinary business records afterwards. Ask us to delete your details and we will.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask for a correction, or ask us to delete it. Write to sales@thecosmiccreator.com.
